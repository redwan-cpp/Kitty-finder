Your cloud drives in Finder, without filling up your Mac.

Kitty Finder is a small Mac menu bar app. It shows your Google Drive in Finder like any other folder, but files only download when you open them. Your Mac keeps its free space.

Right in Finder

Each Google account appears as its own drive. Browse, open, save, rename and drag files as usual.

Downloads on demand

Nothing is copied to your Mac until you open it. Large videos stream instead of downloading in full.

Space stays free

You choose how much space downloaded copies may use. The ones you haven't opened for longest are cleared automatically.

Private by design

Kitty Finder talks to Google directly from your Mac. There is no Kitty Finder server, account or analytics.

How it works

  1. Open Kitty Finder and choose Add Google Drive… in the menu bar.
  2. Sign in with Google and allow access to your Drive files.
  3. Your drive appears in Finder under Kitty Finder. Edits upload in the background.

Kitty Finder asks for access to your Google Drive files because it needs to list, open, save, rename and delete them on your behalf when you do those things in Finder. It never does anything you didn't ask Finder to do. See the privacy policy for details.

Open source engine

Kitty Finder is built on rclone (MIT licence), the widely used open-source tool for cloud storage.
