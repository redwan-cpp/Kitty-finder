Privacy policy

Effective November 6, 2026

In short: Kitty Finder runs entirely on your Mac. Your files and your Google sign-in go only between your Mac and Google. We have no servers that receive your data, and we don't collect analytics.

Who we are

Kitty Finder is a macOS app that shows your Google Drive in Finder. You can contact us at aminmredwan@gmail.com.

What Kitty Finder accesses

When you add a Google account, Kitty Finder asks Google for permission to see, edit, create and delete your Google Drive files (the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive scope). It also reads your account's email address from the Drive API so it can label the drive in Finder.

This permission is needed because Kitty Finder acts as a drive in Finder: when you open, save, rename, move or delete a file there, Kitty Finder makes the same change in your Google Drive.

How your data is used

  • Kitty Finder lists your Drive files so they appear in Finder.
  • It downloads a file's contents only when you or an app on your Mac open that file.
  • It uploads changes you make to files in the Kitty Finder drive.
  • It uses your Google data for nothing else: no advertising, no profiling, no training of AI or machine-learning models, and no sale or sharing.

What is stored, and where

  • Sign-in tokens are stored on your Mac in an encrypted file. The encryption key is kept in your macOS login keychain.
  • Copies of files you open are kept on your Mac so they open quickly next time, up to a size limit you choose. Older copies are removed automatically, and you can remove them all at any time with “Free Up Space”.
  • Mac-only file details (such as Finder tags and folder view settings) are kept on your Mac and are not uploaded.
  • Logs of what the app did (for example, which file was uploaded) are kept on your Mac to help with troubleshooting. They are never sent anywhere automatically.

None of this leaves your Mac except the requests Kitty Finder makes directly to Google to read and change your Drive files.

Sharing

We don't receive your Google data, so we don't share, sell or transfer it to anyone. Your data goes only between your Mac and Google, under Google's privacy policy.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Kitty Finder's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • In Kitty Finder, open Settings → Accounts and choose Remove. This deletes the account's sign-in tokens and downloaded copies from your Mac. Nothing is deleted from Google Drive.
  • You can also revoke Kitty Finder's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the Kitty Finder app and the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Kitty Finder removes everything Kitty Finder stored.

Children

Kitty Finder is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
